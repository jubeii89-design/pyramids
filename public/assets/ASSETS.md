Notes:

- The art was delivered through the chat, which caps images at 2000px on the
  long edge; the files above are those deliveries re-encoded (JPEG quality 82).
  Full-resolution originals from Gemini can replace them at the same names.
- Gemini embeds an invisible SynthID watermark in generated images; it does not
  affect use.
- Earlier themes used a NASA photograph and then an Unsplash photograph
  (Jeremy Thomas); both are removed, and nothing in the game derives from them.
- **No restricted, share-alike or attribution-required material is included
  in this directory.** (The SIL OFL travels with its licence file; it places no
  restriction on the game.)
