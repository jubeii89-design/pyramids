The Word Game Where You Rob Your Friends

Everyone gathers around one screen, phones in hand. Somebody spots a word. They grab a letter off your stack to spell it — and the whole table groans.

Every pyramid you lift uncovers a new letter underneath, so the board keeps shifting and the words keep coming. No math, no waiting, no scorekeeping to slow you down — just spell fast and steal often.

Set up in seconds: one screen, a QR code, and 2–4 players.

▲ The Opening120 pyramids stacked five high. Four colors, one board, and a whole alphabet buried under them.
▲ The ScrambleWords carve holes in the board. Every capture uncovers fresh letters — and new chances to raid.
▲ The FinishEmpty your color to end it. Now the peaks get counted — and everyone finds out who really won.

How to Play

Stack. Steal. Spell to win.

The board

  • 10×10 grid. The outer ring is the Word Runway.
  • The inner squares carry printed letters covered by black house pyramids.
  • Each color starts with 30 pyramids in six stacks of five. Only the top of a stack is in play.

Your turn

  1. Tap the square where your word starts and pick Across or Down.
  2. Type a word of 3+ letters. Matching exposed letters are used in place; other exposed pyramids — yours or your opponents' — slide in to finish it.
  3. Every word must be a real word, use at least one of your own pyramids and one black letter, and move at least one pyramid.
  4. Illegal words are simply rejected — no penalty, try again.

Scoring

  • Every pyramid used in your word is captured and scores the value on its peak (1–9). Printed board letters score nothing.
  • The game ends when a player's color is fully cleared from the board, or after two full rounds of passes.
  • Your pyramids still on the board count against your score. Highest score wins.
Please remember to have fun — but most importantly, always spell to win.
